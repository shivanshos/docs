# Verify Web3 wallet ownership

Current status: **owner-declared, cryptographic verification pending**.

To verify control of the public address `0x4fd036ece52c4b0746da71ecf8be38c94d435164`, use a trusted verifier and sign a human-readable, one-time message. A proper challenge identifies the intended domain and purpose, includes an unpredictable nonce, has an issue time and expires quickly. The signature must be checked against the exact wallet address.

- EVM externally owned accounts may use an EIP-191 message signature.
- Contract accounts may require EIP-1271 signature validation.
- A signed message should **not** request an on-chain transfer or token approval.

Do not share a private key, seed phrase, recovery code, login token, or wallet backup.

Once the signature has been independently validated, record the time, verifier and public proof in the registry. Until then, keep the status as owner-declared, not verified.
